Psychosocial Development Theory

Erik Erikson proposed that personality develops throughout life. Every individual passes through eight psychosocial stages. Each stage presents a conflict (crisis). Successful resolution develops a positive virtue, while failure may create adjustment problems.

Epigenetic Principle

Development follows a fixed sequence. Each stage builds upon the successful completion of the previous stage.

Eight Stages of Psychosocial Development

Stage Age Crisis Virtue
1 0–1 year Trust vs Mistrust Hope
2 1–3 years Autonomy vs Shame & Doubt Will
3 3–6 years Initiative vs Guilt Purpose
4 6–12 years Industry vs Inferiority Competence
5 12–18 years Identity vs Role Confusion Fidelity
6 Young Adulthood Intimacy vs Isolation Love
7 Middle Adulthood Generativity vs Stagnation Care
8 Old Age Integrity vs Despair Wisdom

Educational Implications

  • Create a safe and supportive classroom.
  • Encourage independence.
  • Develop confidence.
  • Recognize individual differences.
  • Help adolescents develop a positive identity.

Memory Trick
